About Home Baked

Learn more about Home Baked, our story and our commitment to providing freshly baked products.

Our Story

Home Baked is a family-owned bakery located in Durban North. The bakery provides freshly baked products for local customers and special occasions.

The business offers a variety of baked goods, including cakes, cupcakes, bread and pastries.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide customers with fresh, quality baked goods while creating enjoyable experiences for everyday occasions and celebrations.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted local bakery recognised for quality products, friendly service and a strong connection with the local community.

Our Values

  • Quality
  • Freshness
  • Customer Satisfaction
  • Creativity
  • Community
